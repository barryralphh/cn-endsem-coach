# Ethernet (IEEE 802.3)

## 10 Golden Points
1. Ethernet is the most popular LAN technology, standard IEEE 802.3, uses CSMA slash CD
2. Frame has seven fields: Preamble 7 bytes, SFD 1 byte, Destination MAC 6, Source MAC 6, Type or Length 2, Data, and CRC 4
3. Preamble is 10101010 repeated seven times for clock sync, SFD is 10101011
4. Data field is minimum 46 bytes and maximum 1500 bytes, padding is added if data is smaller
5. Minimum frame is 64 bytes and maximum frame is 1518 bytes, without preamble and SFD
6. MAC address is 48 bits or 6 bytes, written as 12 hex digits
7. Last bit of first byte: 0 means unicast, 1 means multicast, all F means broadcast
8. Multicast IP to Ethernet MAC: take 01 00 5E, then the lower 23 bits of the IP address
9. In the second octet of the IP, if the value is more than 127, subtract 128 before converting to hex
10. Standard Ethernet 10 Mbps, Fast Ethernet 100 Mbps, Gigabit 1000 Mbps, slot time 51.2 microseconds
