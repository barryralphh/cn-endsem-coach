# TCP Congestion Control

## 10 Golden Points
1. Congestion window cwnd limits how much a sender can send, sender window = min of cwnd and rwnd
2. Slow start: cwnd starts at 1 MSS and doubles every RTT, until it reaches ssthresh
3. Congestion avoidance: cwnd grows by 1 MSS every RTT, this is additive increase
4. Timeout means severe congestion: ssthresh = cwnd divided by 2, then cwnd = 1 and slow start again
5. Three duplicate ACKs mean mild congestion: ssthresh = cwnd divided by 2
6. TCP Tahoe on three duplicate ACKs sets cwnd to 1, TCP Reno sets cwnd = ssthresh and goes to congestion avoidance
7. Fast retransmit resends the lost segment after three duplicate ACKs without waiting for timeout
8. AIMD means additive increase and multiplicative decrease, giving the sawtooth graph
9. Cwnd is counted in MSS units in numericals, never above the maximum cwnd
10. In slow start cwnd sequence is 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and so on, one value per RTT

## Worked calculations

### Scene 1: Slow start = doubling | Congestion avoidance = +1 per RTT
    ## Rules
    slow start: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 ...
    cong. avoid: 33, 34, 35 ...
    ## On congestion
    timeout: ssthresh = cwnd/2
             cwnd = 1
    3 dup ACK: ssthresh = cwnd/2
             Reno: cwnd = ssthresh
    ! Timeout is more severe

### Scene 2: Assumption likho: initial ssthresh = 32. Timeout at 48 -> ssthresh 24
    ## Phase 1 (ssthresh = 32 assumed)
    slow start: 1,2,4,8,16,32
    cong. avoid: 33 ... 48
    ## Timeout at cwnd = 48
    ssthresh = 48 / 2 = 24
    cwnd = 1
    ! restart slow start
    ~ numbers per RTT

### Scene 3: 3 dup ACK at 32 -> ssthresh = 16. Reno: cwnd = 16, Tahoe: cwnd = 1
    ## Phase 2 (ssthresh = 24)
    slow start: 1,2,4,8,16,24 (capped)
    cong. avoid: 25 ... 32
    ## 3 dup ACK at cwnd = 32
    ssthresh = 32 / 2 = 16
    ! Reno: cwnd = 16
    ! Tahoe: cwnd = 1
    ## Then
    17, 18, 19 ... up to max 64

### Scene 4: Convention likho: slow start cwnd ssthresh par ruk jaata hai
